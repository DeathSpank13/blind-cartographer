Blind Cartographer

A browser game about maps and orientation by Jaber Rashki Ghaleh No

You play a courier who arrives in the harbor town of Maya Sol to deliver the five farewell letters of its old mapmaker. His only condition is that they are delivered by someone who has never seen a map of his city, so there is no minimap, no compass and no quest arrow. You find your way by the skyline, the water and the character of each district, and if you want you can draw your own map in a sketchbook. After the last letter the game finally shows you the real map of the city, right next to your own drawing.
