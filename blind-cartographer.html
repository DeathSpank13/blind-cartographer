Blind Cartographer

Maya Sol. The old mapmaker Wade Wilson is dead. He leaves five letters — and one condition in his will: they must be carried by someone who has never seen a map of his city.

That someone is you. You step off the ferry with his satchel. No map. No markers. Only the city itself: its water, its streets, its towers. Learn it the way he did — and if you like, draw what you learn in your sketchbook. At journey's end, we will compare.

M — open or close your sketchbook

H — this help

There is no map and no compass. Watch for tall landmarks above the rooftops, keep track of edges — the river, the waterfront — and learn the character of each district. The letters describe every address by such things.

The Last Map

From the mill hill, Maya Sol lies below you: the harbour, the river, the tower, the plaza. You reach for the next letter and find none — and realise you no longer need to search for anything. Wade was right about the shoes. He was wrong about one thing: every walker draws a map, whether on paper or not.
